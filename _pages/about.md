I am a second-year PhD student in economics at the Bordeaux School of Economics, University of Bordeaux, supervised by [Quentin Stoeffler](https://www.bse.u-bordeaux.fr/membres/quentin-stoeffler/). My thesis is entitled *Targeting Poverty and Resilience in Sub-Saharan African Countries*.

My research is in development economics and focuses on how social protection programs identify the poor in Sub-Saharan Africa. I compare targeting methods such as Proxy Means Testing, community-based targeting and hybrid approaches, looking at both their accuracy and how legitimate they are seen to be by communities. I also explore how machine learning can improve statistical targeting.
