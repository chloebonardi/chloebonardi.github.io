title: "Comparing and Improving Targeting Approaches for Monetary, Health, and Nutrition Deprivation in Burkina Faso"
date: 2025
venue: 'Working Paper'
collection: publications
category: workingpapers
authors: "Chloé Bonardi, Kadidiatou Kadio, Valéry Ridde, Quentin Stoeffler"
excerpt: "We compare Proxy Means Testing and community-based targeting to analyze which method best identifies the poor across monetary, nutritional, and health dimensions, in the context of a health fee exemption program in Burkina Faso. We further explore how statistical targeting performance can be improved using machine learning, and whether the logic underlying community-based selection can be incorporated into statistical approaches."
